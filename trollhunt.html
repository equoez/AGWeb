Orders from the Warden

Trolls pour from the cave-mouth on the far ridge and follow the old supply road down to our gate. Hold the wall, and turn their own plunder into the coin that pays your hunters.

  • Click or tap a troll on the path to strike it with your blade.
  • Slain trolls drop gold. Spend it below on hunters and upgrades.
  • Hunters posted on the wall loose crossbow bolts on their own.
  • Trolls that reach the gate batter the wall. A cyclops leads every fifth wave.
  • If the wall is breached, the trolls plunder a quarter of your gold before the Order drives them back.
